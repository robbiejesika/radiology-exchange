A different choice

The answer shouldn’t have to be “hire another radiologist” or “give up control.”

Traditional teleradiology can solve coverage—but often by moving the relationship, network, and operating model outside your center. We’re exploring a more flexible middle path.

Traditional teleradiology

Outsource the reading function

A single outside organization provides coverage through its own network and operating model.

  • —Vendor-managed network. The provider determines which radiologists are available to you.
  • —Limited relationship continuity. The physician reading today may not be someone your team knows.
  • —Coverage comes as a package. Contracting and pricing are structured around the vendor’s model.
  • —Control is traded for convenience. That may be exactly right for some centers—but not all.
Radiology Exchange concept

Build your own virtual group

Assemble a center-approved network and use technology to make that network easier to operate.

  • ✓Choose your radiologists. Build preferred relationships around specialty, availability, performance, and fit.
  • ✓Keep direct relationships. Your center knows who is reading and can strengthen continuity over time.
  • ✓Define your economics. Professional arrangements stay between the imaging center and radiologist.
  • ✓Add automatic backup. If a preferred reader is unavailable, a study can expand to other center-approved radiologists.
How the concept works

Your network. Made usable.

The platform is designed to reduce the transaction costs that make independent radiologist capacity difficult to access today.

01

Build your network

Find radiologists by state authorization, board certification, fellowship training, subspecialty expertise, objective turnaround metrics, and availability.

02

Approve once

Review a continuously maintained Credential Passport against your center’s own requirements and approve physicians for your network.

03

Route flexibly

Send studies to a preferred reader—or allow them to expand to other approved radiologists when time or capacity requires it.

04

Build continuity

Learn who performs well for your center and build a virtual group that becomes more dependable over time.

Beyond a credential file

Know who is actually eligible to read for your center.

The idea is to move beyond simply storing documents. The platform would compare verified physician information against each center’s predetermined requirements so the center can quickly identify radiologists who appear ready for its approval.

Final credentialing decisions remain with the imaging center. This is a concept-stage illustration, not a representation of currently available functionality.

A deliberately different operating model

Technology infrastructure—not another radiology group.

The concept is intentionally designed around enabling professional relationships rather than replacing them.

The platform would help

Maintain objective credential information, identify physicians who match center-defined requirements, facilitate workflow and communication, surface availability, support routing, and reduce repetitive administration.

The center stays in control

The imaging center selects and approves radiologists, establishes professional relationships and fees, and retains responsibility for its credentialing and communication policies.
